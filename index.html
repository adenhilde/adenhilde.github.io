Signal Metadata Exploit — RTT Side-Channel Research

Implemented an exploit of the packet round-trip time (RTT) metadata leakage vulnerability in Signal Messenger, as documented in the Careless Whisper academic paper (arXiv:2411.11194). The attack exploits Signal's silent delivery receipt mechanism to infer a target's device activity state, daily schedule, number of active sessions, and OS fingerprint — all without generating any notification on the victim's device. Built entirely in shell scripting, integrating signal-cli and socat for raw socket communication.

OnionFlation — Tor PoW Puzzle Inflation Attack Study

Conducting an independent technical study of the OnionFlation attack class (USENIX Security 2025, "Onions Got Puzzled"), a critical vulnerability in Tor's proof-of-work client puzzle defense for onion services. The attack exploits routing asymmetry and Tor's 5-minute DHT update interval to artificially inflate puzzle difficulty for all connecting clients, rendering a targeted onion service unreachable at an attack cost of approximately $1.20 to spike and $0.10/hour to sustain. Analyzing all three documented attack strategies and evaluating proposed mitigations.
